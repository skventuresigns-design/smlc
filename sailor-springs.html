Sailor Springs, Illinois

Est. 1869 | 62879

Experience the rejuvenation of nature

Olney Nearby

Visit nearby Olney to catch a glimpse of the famous white squirrels.

Little Wabash River

The area drains into the Little Wabash River, flowing south through Illinois. Big Muddy Creek also runs nearby, two miles northeast of town. The area has deep historical ties to the oil industry and early railroads.

Local History

Once a booming resort town famous for medicinal healing springs. The historic schoolhouse, built in the late 1890s, remains a landmark of the town's peak era.
