Little Wabash River

Watershed: The area drains into the Little Wabash River. It flows south through Illinois and joins the main Wabash River near the border of White and Gallatin counties.

Nearby Features: Big Muddy Creek is another local waterway two miles northeast of Sailor Springs. The area has historical ties to the oil industry and railroads.

Recreation: While used for fishing and boating, swimming is often not recommended due to water quality concerns. Check USGS or NWS for real-time data.

Local History

Sailor Springs was once a resort town, famous for healing springs. The school, probably built in the 1890s with a "Romanesque" feel, reflects the town's peak era. Many buildings from the 1970s are now gone.

Pro Tip: A historic spot famous for its ghost town feel, springs, and unique roadside attractions.
