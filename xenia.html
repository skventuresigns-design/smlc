Xenia, Illinois

Est. 1853 | 62899

Historic Roots

Xenia's roots run deep in Clay County history. Named after the Greek word for "Hospitality," the town flourished after the arrival of the railroad in 1854. What began as an early hunting ground became a vital community where the work ethic is built on handshakes and local spirit.

Community Park

Support the XFPD at our annual Fish Fry. All funds go directly to the purchase of life-saving safety equipment for our volunteers.
