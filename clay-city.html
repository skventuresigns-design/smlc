Clay City

EST. 1855 | 62824

Home of the Wolves

The Wolf represents the strength and unity of our local community and school spirit. Tradition and pride run deep in the heart of Clay City.

Our Location

Nestled in the heart of Clay County, Clay City serves as a vital link on the prairie, connecting our community to the beauty of the Little Wabash.

Clay City Schools

Dedicated to fostering excellence in education and athletics. Our schools are the foundation upon which the future of our village is built.

Local Attractions

Big Buck Hunting

Nationally recognized for world-class whitetail deer hunting.

Wabash Park

A peaceful community retreat for camping, fishing, and family gatherings.

Clay City Schools

A highly supportive "Pride" environment focused on individual student success.

Historical Heritage

The Pottery Legacy

Local blue-white clay fueled a massive 19th-century pottery industry, establishing a legacy of craftsmanship.

Railroad Hub

Founded in 1855, the town served as a vital strategic stop for the historic Ohio & Mississippi line.

Community & Growth

Annual Traditions

Seasonal festivals and high school sports create a deep, lifelong sense of belonging.

Regional Access

Enjoy the peace of rural living with quick access to healthcare and shopping in nearby Flora and Olney.
