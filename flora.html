Flora Illinois Rich Heritage

Flora, IL was established in 1854 by Samuel White, who donated land for several buildings and the city park.

Flora's Historical Timeline

1854

Founding of Flora

Flora was established as a key stop along the railroad, setting the stage for future growth and development.

1900

Industrial Expansion

The turn of the century brought industrial growth, with new businesses and factories contributing to the town’s prosperity.

1950

Cultural Renaissance

Post-war Flora saw a cultural boom, with new schools and theaters. In 1954 the Centenial Birthday was celebrated.

2000

Modern Revitalization

Flora embraced modernization while preserving its historical charm, leading to a revitalized downtown area.
